## Featured work

### [ShopiLink — Order & Inventory Management](https://inventory.api.shopilinkcambodia.com/login)

Production SaaS for Cambodian wholesale SMEs, built as co-founder and technical lead. It brings multi-branch inventory, orders, invoices, delivery notes, reporting, async exports, and AI-assisted demand insights into one system.

`Next.js` `NestJS` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma` `Docker` `Nginx` `GitHub Actions`

### [Sabay Bike Tour — Booking & Operations](https://sabay-tour-azure.vercel.app/)

Full-stack client platform combining a public bicycle-tour booking journey with a role-based back office, live pricing, email confirmations, finance workflows, audit history, and automated daily closing.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma` `Tailwind CSS`

### [Learnify — AI Learning SaaS](https://learnify-ochre-tau.vercel.app/)

AI-powered learning platform that turns uploaded PDFs into interactive quizzes and flashcards. It includes AI integrations, cloud document storage, authentication, and Bakong KHQR checkout with server-side payment verification.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Groq` `Gemini` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma` `Cloudflare R2`

### MPTC Provincial Website Rebuild

Rebuilt a provincial government website from WordPress to Next.js during my internship at Cambodia's Ministry of Post and Telecommunications, improving page-load speed by **65%**, reducing Time to First Byte by **50%**, and increasing indexed pages by **40%**.
